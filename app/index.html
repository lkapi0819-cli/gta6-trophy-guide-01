How to use this guide

  1. Play the story first (Stage 1) and keep a rolling manual save — seven trophies are missable one-time moments.
  2. Tick trophies off as you earn them. Progress saves in this browser automatically; export it before switching devices.
  3. Chase counters while you travel — stunts, vehicles, areas and choices are nearly free when spread across the whole game.
  4. Turn on spoiler protection if you want story trophy names and descriptions hidden until you earn them.

Roadmap to the Platinum

Seven stages, in the order a first playthrough should attack them. Story first, counters as you travel, co-op and clean-up last.

⚠ Missable trophies — keep a manual save
